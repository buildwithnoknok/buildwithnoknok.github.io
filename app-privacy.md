# noknok App — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 July 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **noknok** mobile application ("the app", "we",
"us", "our") handles information. The app is provided by noknok (Christopher Houben,
Switzerland). If you have any questions, contact us at **christopher@noknok.app**.

## Short version

The noknok app does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers.
There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.
The only sensitive information the app handles — your Wi-Fi network name and password —
is entered by you and sent **directly to your own noknok device** on your local network
so that the device can connect to your Wi-Fi. We never receive or store it.

## What the app does

The noknok app helps you set up and configure noknok modular hardware. To do this it:

- **Downloads public product information** (product catalogues and configuration files)
  from the internet. This is an ordinary download and sends no personal information about
  you.
- **Sends your Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and password to your own noknok device** over
  your local network, so the device can join your Wi-Fi. This information goes only to
  your device — not to noknok or any third party — and is not stored by us.

## Information we collect

**None.** The app does not collect personal data, does not require an account, and
contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

## Wi-Fi credentials

When you set up a device, you may enter your Wi-Fi network name and password. The app
transmits these **only** to the noknok device you are setting up, on your local network.
noknok does not receive, log, or store your Wi-Fi credentials.

## Permissions

The app uses your device's **internet / local network access** solely to (a) download
public product files and (b) communicate with your noknok device during setup. It does
not access your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

## Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

## Children

The noknok app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from
anyone.

## Security

Because the app stores no personal data on our servers, there is no personal data for us
to lose. Communication with your noknok device happens on your own local network.

## Changes to this policy

As we add features (for example, optional user accounts or cloud-connected features), we
will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be
reflected here before the affected features are released.

## Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email **christopher@noknok.app**.
